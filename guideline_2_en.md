# Transcription Guidelines

1. Your only task is to transcribe, or copy, the contents of the provided old newspapers into the Google Sheet “Hasil Transkripsi” (Transcription Results).
2. The assignment of newspapers to be transcribed can be seen in the “Monitoring” sheet.
3. Transcription results are written in the “Hasil Transkripsi” sheet. In that sheet, you need to enter the newspaper name, the publication date in YYYY-MM-DD format (for example, 1960-01-30 means January 30, 1960), the page number you transcribed, and the article title and article content.
4. Each page contains many articles, according to the newspaper being copied.
5. You must ignore advertisements, images along with their captions, and the hyphens “-” that sometimes appear. Example: “pada 3 mei 1946 mereka men- dapati banjak sekali persendja- taan”. Your copy should be: “pada 3 mei 1946 mereka mendapati banjak sekali persendjataan”.
6. If you encounter content that is hard to make out because it is torn, covered by ink, or the scan quality is poor, write it according to your interpretation and add a note in the “keterangan” (notes) column.
